## Findings

### Authorization and local persistence

1. **Offline authorization is a separate policy from server session/token lifetime.** The resolved policy grants up to seven days after successful online authentication; successful online reauthentication resets it, ordinary server-token expiry/network loss/restart do not, and explicit logout cancels it immediately. Expiry and known deactivation lock access while preserving local data. Known deactivation/revocation prevents future authorized work once the client learns of it. Source: `epic-5-context.md`; Story 5.2 spec and closure review; UX `DECISIONS.md` OD-01. **Disposition:** accept as the inherited Epic 6+ authorization boundary.

2. **Authorization context and saved evidence have distinct lifecycles.** Story 5.3 establishes encrypted local drafts, one active draft per employee/task, transactional durable autosave and explicit Save, revision checks, and preservation across authorization-context removal. A task authorization/cache record may be revoked while its draft remains preserved. Source: Story 5.3 spec (including its final closure review), `apps/mobile/local-drafts/model.ts`, `authorized-drafts.ts`, SQLite schema/database, and 5.5 tests in the working tree. **Disposition:** accept; preserve this separation in later work.

3. **Cached task context must be revalidated at authorization boundaries.** The Story 5.5 review surfaced concrete stale-access cases: online cached-draft access needs task authorization revalidation; stale async responses must not reveal protected content; an authoritative successful task-list omission revokes cached task context; a transient/failed refresh is not revocation; reconnect during an asynchronous offline read must reevaluate authorization mode; `TASK_NOT_ASSIGNED` revokes cached context but preserves the draft; and cache cleanup remains scoped to employee/task across logout or account switching. The working-tree App and regression tests implement/exercise scoped cache replacement/revocation, transient-list failure handling, task denial, delayed list/detail/read completions, and draft preservation. Source: `apps/mobile/App.tsx`, `apps/mobile/local-drafts/authorized-drafts.ts`, `sqlite-draft-database.ts`, `App.render.test.tsx`, local-draft tests; supplied Story 5.5 review closure scenarios (no persisted 5.5 report). **Disposition:** accept these checks as release-critical regression coverage for protected local work; carry them into future authorization/cache changes.

### Catalogue and offline field work

4. **The Graphie Mobile catalogue is versioned project configuration with explicit provenance.** It is a PoV catalogue, not a generic form engine or CETEM-approved business-rule source. Unknown CETEM field rules must stay unresolved; implementation should not manufacture them. Source: Story 5.4 spec; `apps/mobile/graphie-pov-catalogue.ts` and tests; Product Owner decision `2026-10-01-dep-01-pov-catalogue.md`; `epics.md`. **Disposition:** accept; calculations/results should consume the catalogue as versioned configuration and retain provenance.

5. **Offline state labels must describe separate facts.** Connectivity, local durability, and server synchronization/acceptance are distinct. Local save or autosave is never evidence of server synchronization or acceptance. Source: Epic 5 Story 5.5 criteria in `epics.md`; French status additions and offline-save/resume tests in the Story 5.5 working-tree diff; UX decision on local save. **Disposition:** accept; retain the explicit French state separation in later workflows.

### Cross-story patterns and process

6. **Asynchronous authorization needs delayed-operation race tests.** Sequential state-transition tests alone cannot demonstrate safety when identity, connectivity, navigation, task-list authority, or denial responses change while local reads/server requests are pending. Story 5.5 adds deferred gates to rendered tests for connectivity transitions during reads, navigation changes, account switching, stale task-list responses, and delayed `TASK_NOT_ASSIGNED` outcomes. Source: `apps/mobile/App.render.test.tsx` working-tree diff and requester-provided closure scenarios. **Disposition:** make delayed-operation races an explicit test-design checkpoint for authorization and local-data stories.

7. **Developer → fresh-context Code Review → narrow closure review worked well.** Story 5.1 and 5.2 records show fresh review findings followed by targeted patches and focused closure checks; Story 5.3 closure records six review remediations, race/isolation coverage, and a bounded final check. This leaves a traceable audit trail and avoids reopening unrelated settled findings. Story 5.5 follows the same reported pattern, though its final report is not persisted as a story artifact. Source: Story 5.1–5.3 specs/closure sections and requester-provided 5.5 closure outcome. **Disposition:** retain the process; persist final closure results in the story artifact.

8. **Scope boundaries and focused regression suites protected the epic sequence.** Story 5.1–5.3 specs explicitly excluded later form/offline/calculation work; 5.4 records catalogue-only boundaries; Story 5.5 tests cover offline field editing without moving calculations, submission, or acceptance forward. Source: each Story 5.1–5.4 spec boundaries and 5.5 working-tree diff. **Disposition:** continue to keep Epic 6+ behavior out of prerequisite stories and maintain focused boundary/regression tests.

9. **Story checkpoints were clear for 5.1–5.4; Story 5.5 artifact durability is a gap.** Existing specs distinguish intent, criteria, explicit exclusions, code maps, verification and review closure. Epic 5 status confirms 5.5 completion, but a durable 5.5 story and closure artifact are absent, so future readers cannot audit its finalized contract and reviewer disposition from the repository alone. **Disposition:** action item to capture Story 5.5 closure evidence in the established artifact format before Story 6.1 planning relies on undocumented details.

## Epic 6 inheritance: calculations and results

- Use the versioned Graphie Mobile catalogue and preserve formula/catalogue/source provenance.
- Keep behavior deterministic and implement only formulas confirmed by `docs/product/graphie-calculation-rules-source-extraction.md`.
- Preserve signed percentage semantics, fixed divisors, referenced readings and denominators, normalized Kerma/mAs reproducibility versus raw-Kerma repeatability, and the explicit 0.49 linearity factor including its `AVERAGE` wrapper.

Sources: `docs/product/graphie-calculation-rules-source-extraction.md`; `epics.md` FR-025–FR-027 and external gates; Story 6.1 criteria in `epics.md`; PRD and architecture AD-7/AD-8.
