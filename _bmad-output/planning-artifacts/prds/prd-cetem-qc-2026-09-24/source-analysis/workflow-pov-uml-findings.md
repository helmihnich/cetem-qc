## Material conflicts and unresolved decisions

1. **Employee administration and task entry scope.** PoV section 5 excludes “Administration complète des employés, responsables, équipes et habilitations” and production authentication/email invitations/temporary-password management; section 9 allows preconfigured accounts. Workflow section 4 starts with Employé creating a control, while UML figure 2 explicitly includes “Gérer employés et tâches” for Responsable and figure 6 gives one Task containing one Audit. User explicitly includes employee management and task creation/assignment. Ask which account lifecycle operations must be real in this PoV versus prepared accounts, and whether an employee can ever create an unassigned control. Do not ask whether employee/task management belongs in Phase 1: user already said it does.

2. **Offline boundary and conflicts.** PoV section 5 excludes “Synchronisation hors ligne entre plusieurs appareils et résolution des conflits.” Workflow section 9 requires offline data entry AND calculations, and on version conflict requires “recharger ou fusionner”; UML section 7 requires a local queue, stable identifiers and conflict resolution. PoV CR-15 requires lossless, duplicate-free resynchronization. Ask whether Phase 1 limits each task to a single employee/tablet with explicit conflict rejection/reload, or includes multi-device work/merge; ask whether submission can be queued offline. User already made offline entry/sync mandatory; do not reopen that.

3. **Post-submission mistake handling and follow-up linkage.** All three freeze submitted measurements/comments. Workflow section 7 says corrections after completion require “un nouvel audit lié”; section 8 says the system “Lie les deux tâches.” PoV D-12 instead says “Une nouvelle intervention crée un nouveau contrôle indépendant.” UML explicitly prohibits a work-validation/rejection step. Ask whether a corrected/new audit must link to its predecessor or remain independent, and how a measurement mistake found after submission but before finalization should proceed without editing the submitted data. These are distinct from machine nonconformity, which is a valid report outcome.

4. **Insight timing and zero-insight completion.** Workflow sections 4–5 generate insights before submission; section 6 says automatic propositions are created “après soumission.” Section 6 retains a “Soumis” insight “Inclus par l’Employé dans l’audit soumis,” and WF-04 tests a manual insight added before submission, despite sections 1 and 8 assigning selection/addition to Responsable after submission. User has settled that Responsable selects/adds. Remaining useful questions: should Employé see deterministic insights before submission or only calculated results; may an all-normal audit be finalized and summarized with zero selected/added insights? The zero-insight question is explicitly open in Workflow section 11 and UML section 7.

5. **Calculation and field rules remain unvalidated dependencies.** PoV section 4 requires a separate calculation/decision catalogue and expressly prohibits inventing absent/ambiguous rules. Section 10 D-01–D-08 leaves geometry formula/tolerance, general-conclusion rule, kVmax, N.A. effect, decimal precision/rounding, strict/inclusive 5/10/15 percent thresholds, linearity/K2 and included readings, and required fields unresolved. Ask whether approved catalogues/reference examples exist, or these should remain named business-validation blockers. D-02 “Conclusion automatique ambiguë” may be a stale reference to automatic overall conformity, since user and UML require human conformity decision; clarify whether there is any deterministic overall assessment separate from the final human decision.

6. **Report modes, signature cycle and limits.** Sources consistently include generated printable Word OR uploaded manual PDF and single immutable official report. PoV D-09–D-11 / Workflow section 11 / UML section 7 still leave exact report/signature contents, configurable PDF limit, and signed-scan reintegration unresolved. Clarify whether scans are out of PoV scope or attached while retaining the immutable official original; a scan cannot silently replace an already immutable official report. Both routes are explicitly documented, so no need to question their inclusion absent a conflicting source.

7. **Evidence attachments and minimal traceability.** Workflow section 5 says “joindre des preuves”; section 6 allows manual-insight proof and requires rule/source/provenance tracing, while PoV section 12 says attachments/report-version governance still must be decided. PoV section 5 excludes a complete audit log but section 8 S7 requires provenance for propositions, additions, AI inputs, draft and final summary. Ask only about concrete attachment scope/types and required minimum event evidence if other sources do not resolve them; distinguish this minimum traceability from excluded production operations/auditing.

8. **Human work validation remnants.** PoV section 1 says Responsable “valide ou non le travail,” section 3 report preconditions say “Après validation,” and Workflow section 4 step 6 has output “Décision sur le travail.” But PoV CR-13 explicitly requires no work validation/rejection stage, and UML introduction/section 7 explicitly prohibits one. Present as a source inconsistency; latest user flow and UML support omitting that stage, but user requested asking about material conflicts rather than silently cleaning them up.

9. **Acceptance baseline incomplete.** PoV section 12 references CR-01 to CR-11 despite additional CR-13/15/16 covering direct conformity decision, offline sync, printing.
